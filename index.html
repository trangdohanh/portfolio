About me

I'm a product designer based in Ontario, with an interest in design systems and frontend development. I leverage the human-centered approach to design meaningful and intuitive interfaces, improving digital experiences one interaction at a time.

Puzzles (like Sudoku and mechanical ones) and problem-solving have always interested me, especially learning how different pieces fit into the overarching system. I like taking something apart—whether it's a pain point in the user journey or a digital component, learning how it works, then trying to make something newer and better with the parts. This sparked my enjoyment in making Figma prototypes and components, working with design systems, and learning more about frontend.

In my free time, you might see me catching up on some fantasy or thriller novels, movies, shows, and some drawings (with headphones on, music playing, and a cup of something sweet).
