About me

I'm a product designer based in Ontario, interested in design systems and frontend development. I leverage the human-centred approach to design meaningful experiences and intuitive interfaces, solving problems one interaction at a time.

Problem-solving has always interested me. I like taking something apart, digitally, learning how it works, then trying to make something slightly new with the parts. This sparked my enjoyment in making Figma prototypes and components, working with design systems, and learning more about frontend.

In my free time, you might see me catching up on some fantasy or thriller novels, movies, shows, and some drawings (with headphones on, music playing, and a cup of something sweet).
