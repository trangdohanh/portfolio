Developing a smart documentation tool for early childhood educators

Overview

Neurodiversity is rising in today's classrooms, but existing support systems for educators often prioritize administrative needs over practical and real-time support. Fragmented support puts responsibility on already-overworked teachers to put together their own resources. Through extensive research and conversations with early childhood educators, our team saw a lack of accessible professional development, inefficient behavioural tracking, and heavy administrative load. We designed NOTO to accelerate documentation and provide practical training modules, with the following key features: professional development dashboard, classroom management tools, smart documentation, and AI assistant.

NOTO was developed as part of Red Thread Innovations' Purposeful Innovation mentorship and came in second place!

Team

Duration

Jan – Apr 2025 (14 weeks)

I led the development of the smart documentation tool to streamline notetaking and reports, allowing educators to reclaim lost time and reduce redundancy.

65% of teachers are unsatisfied with their current documentation system, citing inefficiency and a high learning curve. As a result, documentation is often time-consuming, inconsistent, and often inaccessible across platforms. In response, we designed the NOTO mobile platform to allow teachers to quickly voice-record or jot down notes. These rough notes are then distilled into structured outputs for report cards, logs, or communication with caregivers.
