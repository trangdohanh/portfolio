Analysis vs Futura Example

Futura, designed by Paul Renner, is considered an archetypical geometric sans-serif typeface. It is one of the inspirations behind the development of Avenir, which was no secret considering“avenir” is French for “future”. While there are similarities like the stemless uppercase U and the round tittle, Avenir and Futura are distinguishable from each other. Frutiger paired a double-story a with a single-story g instead of having both being single-story like Renner did. The stroke weight of Avenir is narrower than Futura’s. The x-heights of both fonts are relatively equal, but Futura has a higher cap-height. Avenir has shorter ascenders and descenders. The characters in Avenir are generally wider, less perfectly geometric, and spaced out more compared to Futura. Because of the flat apexes and vertexes, they feel more uniform.

AVENIR versus FUTURA

There are other decisions Frutiger made that distinguish Avenir from Futura while still reinforcing its uniformity and geometry. The lowercase e and g have wider apertures. The lowercase j, t, and y have an arc. The lowercase u has a stem while the uppercase does not, whereas both forms in Avenir are stemless. The right side of the uppercase G is flat instead of being rounded. The uppercase M has straight parallel verticals. The circles in the uppercase O and Q feel more rounded compared to the same letters in Futura. The leg of the capital R is offset from the stem, opening the counter. The tail of the capital Q lies flatly on the baseline instead of crossing the circle. The numbers 5 and 7 seem to be set in a more upright position. The feet of the numbers 7 and 9 lie flatly on the baseline instead of being angled. The stroke weight of Avenir is narrower than Futura’s.
